Urgent Exams

Entrance Examination Date Announced for Diploma Programs

Published: Shrawan 7, 2082 1,248 views Examination Office

The Council for Technical Education and Vocational Training (CTEVT) hereby announces that the entrance examination for all diploma-level programs will be conducted on Shrawan 25, 2082.

All applicants are required to download their admit cards from the student portal and bring a printed copy along with a valid photo ID to the examination center.

  • Reporting time: 9:00 AM
  • Examination duration: 2 hours
  • Total marks: 100 (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English)

For further queries, contact the examination office during working hours.
