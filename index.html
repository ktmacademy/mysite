04Questions

Before you enrol

If your question is not here, message us on WhatsApp — that is usually the fastest way to get an answer.

Are classes online or in person?
Classes are taught live in small groups. Message us on WhatsApp for the current format and timings before you enrol, as these change between terms.
What does the fee cover?
AI for Everyone is NPR 6,000 for the full course. Class 12 NEB tuition is NPR 3,000 per subject, per month. Those are the full amounts — there is no separate registration or material charge.
Do I have to pay to use the videos or the app?
No. All 135 video lessons on YouTube are free and need no sign-up, and the CTEVT Plus app is free on Google Play. Only the live classes above are paid.
Which CTEVT semesters do you cover?
The free video courses cover the first and second semesters end to end, along with bridge courses in Math, Physics, Chemistry, English and Biology. The app carries notes and past papers for more programs and semesters than the videos do.
Can I get the notes without joining a class?
Yes. Download CTEVT Plus on Google Play and the notes, past papers and syllabus are there for free. Joining a class gets you a teacher, not access to materials.
How do I enrol?
Message us on WhatsApp or call 9868004742. Tell us the subject and we will confirm the next batch and how to pay.
You do not teach the subject I need. What now?
Use the form below to tell us the subject and what you would expect to pay. We read every request when planning the next term.
